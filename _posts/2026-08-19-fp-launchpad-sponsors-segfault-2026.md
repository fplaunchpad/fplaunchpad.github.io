date: 2026-08-19

FP Launchpad is proud to sponsor [SegFault 2026](https://segfault.compilertech.org/#tracks), an online hackathon on compilers and programming languages running through 3 October 2026, with an in-person finale at IISc Bengaluru. The hackathon includes a dedicated Functional Programming theme, for projects built with OCaml, Haskell, and type systems.
