FP Launchpad is accepting applications for the second cohort of its Post-Baccalaureate Fellowship. We are looking for **8 Fellows** to join us at [IIT Madras](https://www.iitm.ac.in/) for a fully funded, two-year research fellowship **starting in 2027**. **Applications close on 15 December 2026.**

Six fellows have joined our first cohort. Our [September update](/blog/2026-09-fp-launchpad-update/) introduces them and describes the research, projects, teaching, and community activities underway at FP Launchpad. For more on the fellowship's aims, mentorship, and paths into graduate study and industry, see the [original fellowship announcement]({% post_url 2026-03-06-applications-open-post-bacc-fellowship %}).

## Who should apply

We are looking for programmers interested in systems and functional programming research, primarily using OCaml and OxCaml. Strong C/C++ skills and evidence of building beyond coursework are expected: open-source contributions, personal projects, research prototypes, or a difficult bug you tracked down. Prior experience with OCaml, Haskell, or Rust is a plus, but not required.

Fellows work **full-time on campus at IIT Madras**, contributing to open-source systems and working towards research publications. You should be able to explain the code you have written and the decisions behind it.

## Funding

Fellows receive a monthly stipend of **up to ₹1,00,000**, based on qualifications and experience, under [IITM IC&SR](https://icandsr.iitm.ac.in/) norms. Conference registration, travel, and accommodation are covered for accepted papers or talks at approved conferences, subject to IITM norms. Relocation, housing, healthcare, and other expenses are not covered separately.

## How to apply

Submit your application by **15 December 2026**, including your motivation, technical background, programming experience, and a code sample you have written from scratch. We look for evidence of understanding and independent work; applications relying primarily on AI-generated responses or code will be rejected.
