FP Launchpad is accepting applications for the second cohort of its Post-Baccalaureate Fellowship. We are looking for **8 Fellows** to join us at [IIT Madras](https://www.iitm.ac.in/) for a fully funded, two-year research fellowship **starting in Summer 2027**.

Our [September update](/blog/2026-09-fp-launchpad-update/) introduces the six fellows in our first cohort and their projects, along with our teaching and community activities. The [original fellowship announcement]({% post_url 2026-03-06-applications-open-post-bacc-fellowship %}) describes the fellowship's aims, mentorship, and paths into graduate study and industry.

None of the candidates shortlisted for our first cohort were women. To help address this, **two of the eight fellowship places in this cohort are reserved for women**. We encourage women interested in systems and functional programming research to apply. **Applications close on 10 December 2026.** We have chosen this date in honour of [Ada Lovelace's birthday](https://blog.sciencemuseum.org.uk/ada-lovelace-visionary-of-the-computer-age/).

<div style="clear: both;"></div>

## Who should apply

We are looking for programmers interested in systems and functional programming research, primarily using OCaml and OxCaml. Strong C/C++ skills are expected, along with work beyond coursework, such as open-source contributions, personal projects, research prototypes, or a difficult bug you tracked down. Prior experience with OCaml, Haskell, or Rust is a plus, but not required.

Fellows work **full-time on campus at IIT Madras**, contributing to open-source systems and working towards research publications. You should be able to explain the code you have written and the decisions behind it.

## Funding

Fellows receive a monthly stipend of **up to ₹1,00,000**, based on qualifications and experience, under [IITM IC&SR](https://icandsr.iitm.ac.in/) norms. Conference registration, travel, and accommodation are covered for accepted papers or talks at approved conferences, subject to IITM norms. Relocation, housing, healthcare, and other expenses are not covered separately.

## How to apply

Apply by **10 December 2026**. Tell us about your motivation, technical background, and programming experience, and include a code sample you have written from scratch.

We look for evidence of understanding and independent work. Applications relying primarily on AI-generated responses or code will be rejected.
