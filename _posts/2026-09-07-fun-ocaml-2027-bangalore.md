title: "FP Launchpad to host FUN OCaml 2027 in Bangalore"
date: 2026-09-07
---

FP Launchpad is hosting [FUN OCaml 2027](https://fun-ocaml.com/), the first edition of FunOCaml, or any major OCaml conference, in India. The two-day event runs 25-26 January 2027 at the MLR Convention Centre, Bangalore, with talks and a keynote on day one and workshops and collaborative hacking on day two. The [call for proposals](https://cfp.fun-ocaml.com/fun-ocaml-2027-bengaluru/cfp) is open to speakers of all experience levels through 30 September.
