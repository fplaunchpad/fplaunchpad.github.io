Programming Languages Winter School 2026

{{ school.dates }} · {{ school.venue }}

This five-day school introduces programming languages, program verification, and runtime systems through lectures and exercises.

Apply to attend: Applications will open soon. We will review your application and let you know whether you have a seat.

Audience

The school welcomes senior undergraduates, graduate students, research staff, faculty, and industry professionals in research positions. Prior study of programming languages, functional programming, or proof assistants is not required.

Programming experience and discrete mathematics are the core foundations for the school. Theory of computation or a Logic for Mathematics and Computer Science (LMC) course is strongly recommended. Data structures and algorithms are useful preparation. Compilers and operating systems are helpful additional background, but are not required.

The discrete mathematics background should include logic and reading and writing basic proofs. Equivalent self-study or work experience is welcome.

Timetable

The timetable is provisional. All times are India Standard Time (UTC+5:30). Each lecture lasts 80 minutes, including questions.

Breaks: 10:20–10:40 and 15:20–15:40. Lunch: 12:00–14:00.
